Who goes into the brain?

Include
Conversations with this sender go into the brain unread, also when an excluded sender is on them: you don’t write confidential things with them on the conversation.
Partial
The AI reads every conversation this sender is on, whoever else is on it, and leaves out what doesn’t belong.
Exclude
Conversations with only excluded senders stay out, and nobody reads them. Include wins: with an included sender on it, a conversation goes in anyway.

The AI’s advice is in pencil, your choice is in ink. A sender you leave alone keeps the advice; you can change any of them. A company where everyone is included, or everyone excluded, is folded into one line: click its name to see who is in it.
